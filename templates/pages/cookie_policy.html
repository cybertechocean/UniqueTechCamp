What Are Cookies & Why Do We Use Them?

Cookies are minute text files downloaded to your device when you browse websites. At UniqueTechCamp, we utilize cookies and web storage to provide essential security, preserve your dark/light UI preferences, remember chat state in our AI lead qualification widgets, and evaluate site performance.

01. Categories of Cookies We Deploy

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Mandatory

Indispensable for core site security, CSRF token verification on Django forms, session state persistence, and load balancing. The site cannot function without these cookies.

Functional & AI Interaction Cookies

Experience

Maintains state across our interactive 24/7 lead qualification chatbots, keeping your service selections active as you move between pages so you don't lose typed prompts or contact answers.

Analytics & Performance Cookies

Performance

Aggregates anonymous telemetry data regarding page load latency, scroll depth, and search queries. This allows our engineers to identify and optimize sluggish pages.

Marketing & Conversion Pixels

Targeting

Tracks the efficacy of our advertising campaigns (such as Google Ads or Meta Ads) to determine which services are receiving high interest, preventing repeated irrelevant ads.

02. How Long Do Cookies Persist?

  • Session Cookies: Temporary files that remain active only while your browser tab is open, vanishing automatically once you close the window.
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain stored on your device for a predetermined lifespan (ranging from 30 days to 12 months) unless manually purged earlier via your browser settings.

03. Managing & Revoking Your Cookie Preferences

You have complete autonomy over your device storage. Most modern browsers let you view, delete, or block cookies across all websites:

Google Chrome Settings → Privacy
Safari Preferences → Security
Mozilla Firefox → Tracking
Microsoft Edge → Permissions

Note: If you disable all cookies including essential session cookies, certain interactive areas of our site (such as client inquiry forms and instant bot calculators) may experience degraded functionality.
